Dodge & Survive

Survive as long as you can. One hit closer to game over.

Game

Controls: WASD / Arrow keys to move, P to pause, R to restart

Description & Instructions

Dodge & Survive is a 2D arcade-style survival game. You control a small player square and must dodge incoming hazards for as long as possible. As you survive, the game becomes harder: enemies spawn faster and move quicker, and the environment changes.

How to Play

  • Use WASD or Arrow keys to move the player.
  • Avoid all moving hazards – each hit costs you one life.
  • You start with 3 lives. When they’re gone, it’s Game Over.
  • Survive long enough to reach Level 2 and watch the environment change.
  • Press P to pause/unpause, R or click Restart to play again.
